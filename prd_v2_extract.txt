1. Executive Summary
LUCE is an AI-powered campaign creation platform initially designed for Shopify, e-commerce and DTC physical-product
brands. The user provides a product URL or product images and basic information. LUCE understands the product, researches
relevant market context, builds a campaign strategy, and generates a complete marketing campaign around it.
CORE CONCEPT
One product → One research-backed campaign → Everything needed to market it.

3. Problem Statement
Small e-commerce and DTC brands often have a product but do not have professional product photography, video production,
UGC creators, designers, copywriters, a full creative team or enough time to create content regularly.
Current workflows may require multiple AI tools: one for product images, another for UGC, another for video, and others for
captions and ads. This creates a fragmented workflow.
Critical problem: AI product distortion. AI-generated product visuals can change the actual product, including bottle shape,
logo, label, packaging, cap, colors and proportions. A beautiful AI image is still unusable if it no longer accurately represents the
real product.
Strategic problem: disconnected content. Individual assets may look good but fail to communicate one consistent campaign
or connect to a clear audience, message or marketing objective.
4. LUCE Solution
LUCE treats the actual product as the central source of truth. The Campaign Agent understands the product, identifies missing
information, conducts relevant research, develops a campaign strategy, and creates multiple marketing assets around the same
product and campaign context.

10. Product Fidelity
CHANGE THE SCENE, NOT THE PRODUCT.
LUCE should preserve important product characteristics including shape, proportions, logo, label, packaging, colors, cap, and
other recognizable details. The generated asset should still clearly represent the user's real product. When a generation cannot
reliably preserve a critical detail, the system should avoid presenting the output as a faithful product depiction.
11. Campaign Consistency
All generated assets should use the same confirmed product information, campaign strategy, audience, positioning, messaging
and creative direction. The goal is one coherent brand campaign, not a collection of unrelated AI outputs.
12. Campaign Storage
Campaigns should be saved so users can revisit them. Firebase is the planned database and storage foundation.

The Campaign Agent is the orchestration layer: it coordinates product understanding, questions, research, strategy and
generation. Individual AI models/services may support specific tasks behind the scenes, but the user experiences one
connected workflow.

20. Future Phase — Social Platform Integration
Potential future capabilities include connecting Instagram, TikTok and Facebook accounts, scheduling content, publishing posts
and managing a content calendar. These features are not part of the MVP.
FUTURE WORKFLOW
CAMPAIGN → CONNECT SOCIAL → PLAN CALENDAR → SCHEDULE → PUBLISH → REVIEW PERFORMANCE
21. Future Phase — SaaS Expansion
The initial product is focused on physical consumer products. SaaS, digital products and other business categories may be
evaluated after the physical-product workflow is validated.
22. Future — Multiple AI Providers
The internal architecture may use different AI services for research, image generation, video generation or other tasks. A
user-facing AI provider marketplace is not an MVP requirement.
LONG-TERM ARCHITECTURE
One Campaign Agent coordinates specialized capabilities while maintaining shared product and campaign context.
23. Key Differentiator & Positioning
LUCE should not position itself as simply another AI image, video or UGC generator. Its central difference is the connected
workflow: Product → Research → Strategy → Campaign.
  Research-first workflow: campaign decisions are informed by relevant market and competitor context.
  Complete campaign: deliver a coordinated set of assets rather than isolated generations.
  Product fidelity: preserve the real product as the source of truth.
  Campaign consistency: keep the audience, message and creative direction aligned.
  One workflow: reduce the need to move between separate tools.
